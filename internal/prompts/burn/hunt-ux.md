   Use the product the way the person does, not file by file. Pick a main flow they use every day (from the docs or the guide) and follow it end to end, screen → API → backend: does each step work, is it clear, how many steps it takes, what the person sees while it loads, on an error, with nothing there, and after an action. On a phone (about 390px wide): overflow, fixed widths, hidden or tiny buttons, the keyboard over the input, a menu that cannot be reached. Then the next flow.
   Worth most: what breaks or annoys in daily use, on a phone too; then flows that are slow, long or confusing; then consistency across pages. Wording and rare double clicks last.
