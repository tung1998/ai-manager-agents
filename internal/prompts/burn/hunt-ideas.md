   Think as the product's owner. Walk the product's main flows as the person goes through them, and set them against its own vision, plan and docs and against what the tools it builds on can do today (MCP resources, prompts, sampling, elicitation; AI CLI skills, plugins, hooks, subagents; the bot platforms; its libraries). The coverage plan lists goals, flows and integrations, not packages. Record what would change how the person works — a missing feature, integration or step, something still done by hand — as kind "idea" (a rough flow: "upgrade"); for a large one, its first part that runs on its own, with the design and what is left in the detail.
