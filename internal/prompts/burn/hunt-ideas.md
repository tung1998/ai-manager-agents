   Think as the product's owner, not as a bug hunter. Compare what the project offers with its own vision, plan and docs, and with what the tools it builds on can do today (for example the MCP spec: resources, prompts, sampling, elicitation; the AI CLIs: skills, plugins, hooks, subagents; the bot platforms; the libraries it uses). Look for a gap: a feature, an integration or a flow that would change how the person works, or something done by hand that could run on its own.
   Pick the one with the most value for its size, and build it (kind "idea"): a large one, its first part that runs on its own, with the design and what is left recorded in the project's docs.
